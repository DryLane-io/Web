The Dry Stack

Database, queue and serializer in one process. Answers in microseconds.

Most backends are three systems taped together: a database to store data, a message queue to move it and a serializer to put it on the wire. Every seam costs a copy, a serialization pass and a network hop. The Dry Stack removes the seams. One process on one box ingests tens of millions of records a second, and you don't need a cluster to be fast.

Measured against the field
8.7× DuckDB's ingest rate. We ran on 1 core, DuckDB got 32. 35× faster history replay than NATS JetStream, on the same box. 16 µs round-trip where gRPC takes 179 µs. Same machine, same messages. 0.87 ns to serialize a 16-byte message, with zero allocations.

Rivals get their fastest settings and every core. Medians from an open harness, and the losses are published too: Facts stores 3.8× more bytes per point than DuckDB, because it keeps columns raw for speed. Click a number for the full table.
